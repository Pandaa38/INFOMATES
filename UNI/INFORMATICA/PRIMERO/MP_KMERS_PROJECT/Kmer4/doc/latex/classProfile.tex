Gets the distance between this \mbox{\hyperlink{classProfile}{Profile}} object ( $P_1$) and the given argument object {\ttfamily other\+Profile} ( $P_2$). The distance between two Profiles $P_1$ and $P_2$ is calculated in the following way\+: 

$d = \frac{ \sum_{kmer_i(P_1)} | rank_{kmer_i(P_1)}^{P_1} - rank_{kmer_i(P_1)}^{P_2} | }{size(P_1) * size(P_2) }$,

where $kmer_i(p_j)$ is the kmer $i$ of the \mbox{\hyperlink{classProfile}{Profile}} $p_j, j \in \{1, 2\}$ and $rank_{kmer_i(p_j)}^{p_k}$ is the ranking of the kmer $i$ of the \mbox{\hyperlink{classProfile}{Profile}} $p_j, j \in \{1, 2\}$ in the \mbox{\hyperlink{classProfile}{Profile}} $p_k$.

The rank of a kmer is the position in which it appears in the list of \mbox{\hyperlink{classKmerFreq}{Kmer\+Freq}}. We consider 0 as the first position (rank equals to 0). When calculating $rank_{kmer_i(P_1)}^{P_2}$, if the kmer $kmer_i(P_1)$ does not appears in the \mbox{\hyperlink{classProfile}{Profile}} $P_2$ we consider that the rank is equals to the size of \mbox{\hyperlink{classProfile}{Profile}} $P_2$. Normalizes the Kmers of the vector of \mbox{\hyperlink{classKmerFreq}{Kmer\+Freq}} in this object. That is, for each \mbox{\hyperlink{classKmer}{Kmer}} in the vector, all its characters are converted to uppercase. Then, invalid characters are replaced by the MISSING\+\_\+\+NUCLEOTIDE value. 

If after the previous normalization process of every kmer, identical kmers are obtained, these will be merged into the first identical kmer by adding their frequencies. For example, suposse the following list of kmers\+: 4 Ct 5 hG 4 nG 1 cT 4

After the process of normalization of every kmer, we obtain the following list of kmers\+:

4 CT 5 \+\_\+G 4 \+\_\+G 1 CT 4

The final step will transform the list into\+: 2 CT 9 \+\_\+G 5
